Myodyssey

A Muscle-Driven Motion Generalist Trained through a Differentiable Musculoskeletal World Model

Yifei YuanXianlian Zhou
New Jersey Institute of Technology
Papersoon Codesoon BibTeX

Abstract

Motion tracking has become the main route to general whole-body control, and generalist policies trained on hundreds of hours of motion capture now reproduce a wide range of human movements. Musculoskeletal models generate motion through the muscles and can estimate muscle activity and joint loads, but training them at this scale remains limited by the cost of simulating and controlling hundreds of redundant muscles. We present Myodyssey, a muscle-driven motion generalist for a 354-muscle full-body model, trained by backpropagation through a differentiable musculoskeletal world model that computes muscle forces and joint torques analytically and learns only the skeletal and contact dynamics. Trained on 182 h of motion capture, one policy tracks the motions of unseen people, then adapts to stairs, ramps, seats and boxes with a local heightmap.
